Privacy Policy

Manabi Do — Last updated: July 1, 2025

Manabi Do is a Japanese learning app. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app does not require an account and does not transmit any information to external servers.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Your spaced-repetition (SRS) practice progress
  • App settings and preferences

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please open an issue on GitHub.
