Difficulty ramp and mastery pills

The ramp is theme-invariant: the same hue in light and dark. Mastery reuses it backwards, so a pill's colour is a ramp entry drawn at 12% over the card. That composite is what the contrast column measures, which is why these are the worst offenders in the app.
